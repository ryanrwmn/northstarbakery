Our story

Made with care, shared with community.

A neighborhood bakery

North Star Bakery is built around the simple pleasure of sharing good food. We bring together familiar bakery favorites and carefully prepared breads and pastries in a welcoming neighborhood setting.

Whether you are stopping in for your usual morning loaf or planning treats for a celebration, we want every visit to feel special.

Thoughtful ingredients

We focus on dependable ingredients and straightforward recipes, choosing quality staples and seasonal ingredients when available. Our goal is to let the flavor and care in each bake speak for itself.

The people behind the bread

Our small bakery team brings together bakers, counter staff, and order helpers. From preparing dough before opening to helping customers choose a treat, each person contributes to a welcoming neighborhood experience.
